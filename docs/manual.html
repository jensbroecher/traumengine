Traum Engine Manual

Traum Engine is a playful, Dreams-inspired 3D Signed Distance Field (SDF) & Painterly Fleck creation engine. Sculpt warm tactile clay coated in living brushstrokes, Scope In to build custom Toy Props, Live-Clone creations across your island, link multi-part puppets with spring and hinge joints, record animations live, and jump straight into Play Mode with zero load times.

Quick Start

Run Traum Engine from the workspace root using Cargo:

# Launch Traum Engine (opens directly onto the editable Floating Diorama Island)
cargo run --bin traum-engine

# Run all CPU unit tests across workspace crates
cargo test

# Run the headless GPU compute + render verification self-test
cargo run --bin traum-engine -- --self-test
Tip: Traum Engine never drops you into an empty grey grid. On launch, you land on a sunlit Floating Diorama Island made of real editable SDF clay with example Toy Props already in place—your very first click can carve a cave, build a hill, or grab a toy.

Core Concepts

World Clay vs. Toy Props

The island terrain lives in the World Canvas (world_edits). Movable, reusable, animatable objects are Toy Props (SceneProp) backed by shared SculptAsset edit lists.

Non-Destructive CSG Edits

Every brushstroke is stored as an ordered SculptEdit (Add, Carve, or Paint) rather than baked triangles—giving you clean undo/redo and compact .ron scene files.

Surface-Magnet 3D Imp

Your 3D cursor—the Imp—glides along existing clay surfaces and projects a vertical ground drop-shadow guide when sculpting in open air so 3D depth is always clear.

Hybrid Clay + Living Flecks

Low looseness keeps a watertight SDF clay core free of pinholes; higher looseness billows surface Flecks outward into painterly grass, clouds, embers, or 12fps stop-motion strokes.

The Four Creative Modes

Switch modes anytime using the top mode pills, the Radial Pie Wheel, or number keys 1–4:

1 Sculpt

Add or carve clay on the World Island or inside a Scoped-In Toy Prop using 6 primitive shapes and smooth CSG blending.

2 Paint

Re-coat existing clay with new colors, looseness, and Living Fleck styles without changing the sculpted shape.

3 Animate

Select, move, rotate, scale, live-clone, attach jointed limbs, assign Motion Gadgets, and record live puppet performances.

4 / Tab Play!

Drop directly onto the island as a bouncy playable character. Walk, jump, ride moving platforms, and explore your creation.

Sculpting & Painting

Brush Operations

  • Add Clay (SculptOp::Add): Smoothly blends new clay onto surfaces or into open air.
  • Carve (SculptOp::Subtract): Cuts tunnels, caves, windows, or dents out of clay. Quick-carve anytime with Alt + Left Drag (or L2 + R2 on controller).
  • Coat / Paint (SculptOp::Paint): Recolors and restyles existing surfaces without altering geometry.

6 Primitive Shapes

Cycle shapes with F, the Shape pill, or Y/Triangle on a controller:

Sphere Soft Box (RoundedBox) Capsule Cylinder Cone Ring (Torus)

Brush Controls & Symmetry

  • Brush Size: Drag the Brush Size slider, hold Shift + Scroll, press [ / ], or pull R2 analog trigger gently/deeply on controller.
  • Soft Clay Blend: Drag the Soft Clay Blend slider or hold Ctrl + Scroll to control how smoothly strokes melt together.
  • Mirror Symmetry (M): Mirrors strokes across the X axis. When Scoped In to a Toy Prop, symmetry automatically uses the Toy's local X axis!

Toy Props, Scope In, Live Cloning & Puppets

Creating & Scoping Into a Custom Toy

  • Press P (or click + New Toy) to spawn a fresh editable Toy Prop.
  • Scope In: Hover a Toy Prop and press Enter or V (or click Scope In / use the Outliner's Edit Clay button). While Scoped In, your brush edits that Toy's local 64³ SDF asset instead of the world island.
  • Scope Out: Press Enter, V, or Esc to return to the island.

Live Clone vs. Make Unique

  • Live Clone (C or Ctrl+D): Duplicates a Toy Prop while sharing its underlying SculptAsset. Sculpting or painting inside any clone updates all linked copies simultaneously!
  • Make Unique (U): Forks a linked clone into its own independent SculptAsset so you can customize it without affecting its siblings.

3D Transform Handles (Animate Mode 3)

  • Drag Prop Body: Moves the Toy Prop in 3D space.
  • Turquoise Ring: Drag to rotate the selected Toy Prop.
  • Gold Orb: Drag to uniformly scale the selected Toy Prop (or use [ / ]).
  • Delete Prop: Press Delete or Backspace to remove the selected Toy Prop.

Multi-Part Puppet Connectors (L or + Attach Limb)

Attach child limbs to any selected Toy Prop to build articulated puppets and contraptions. Cycle connector behavior with the connector pill:

Connector Behavior
Spring Ball (BallSpring) Bouncy secondary-motion ball joint that wobbles and lags organically behind parent movement.
Spin Hinge (HingeSpin) Continuous spinning hinge around local Y—ideal for windmills, propellers, and spinning hats.
Swing Hinge (HingeSwing) Rhythmic swinging hinge around local Z—ideal for wings, arms, tails, and hanging lanterns.
Rigid Weld (Rigid) Welded attachment that follows the parent's pose 1:1.

Animation & Motion Gadgets

Zero-Keyframe Motion Gadgets

Cycle a procedural Motion Gadget on any selected Toy Prop for instant life without recording keyframes:

Still (None) Hover (HoverBob) Spin Hop (BouncyHop) Swing (Pendulum)

Live Action Recorder (R)

  1. Select a Toy Prop in Animate mode (3).
  2. Press R (or click Record) to arm the Action Recorder.
  3. Grab and move/wiggle the Toy Prop live—Traum Engine records your ActionClip keyframes and loops your performance automatically.
  4. Use Clear Clip to remove a recorded loop if you want to re-record.

Instant Play Mode

Press 4, Tab, or Start (controller) at any moment to drop onto your island with the playable bouncy character buddy.

  • Zero Load Time: Collisions are resolved directly against the live SDF world and Toy Props.
  • Moving Platforms: Stand on animated or Gadget-driven Toy Props and ride along with them.
  • Forgiving Feel: Includes coyote-time jumping and squash-and-stretch feedback.
  • Return to Editor: Press Tab or Esc to snap right back into Sculpt mode.

Toy UI, Radial Pie Wheel & Outliner

8-Slice Bouncy Radial Pie Wheel

Hold Space and flick your mouse (release to confirm), tap the top Wheel button on touchscreens, or hold L1/LB + flick Right Stick on a controller:

8 Quick Slices:

  1. Add Clay — Build smooth clay onto surfaces
  2. Carve — Cut tunnels, caves, or dents
  3. Paint — Color & restyle without changing shape
  4. Scope In / Out — Edit inside a Toy Prop
  5. Copy Toy — Live-Clone linked copies
  6. + Limb — Attach a jointed child part
  7. Symmetry — Toggle Mirror X sculpting
  8. Play! — Jump into Play Mode

Tactile Sliders & Scene Objects Outliner

  • Sliders: Brush Size, Soft Clay Blend, Painterly Fluffiness (Looseness), and Miniature Camera Blur (Bokeh DOF).
  • Scene Objects Outliner: Located on the right side of the viewport. Shows the World Canvas plus a hierarchical parent/child tree of all Toy Props, with quick buttons to focus, Move, or Edit Clay (Scope In), plus pagination for large scenes.

Saving, Loading & Undo

  • Undo / Redo: Ctrl+Z or Z to Undo; Ctrl+Y to Redo (supports strokes, prop spawning, deletion, cloning, and scene resets).
  • Save Scene (Ctrl+S or F5): Saves your island, custom Toy Props, connectors, and recorded animations to island_dream.ron.
  • Load Scene (F9): Reloads island_dream.ron from disk.
  • Reset Island: Restores the default Floating Diorama Island (undoable).
